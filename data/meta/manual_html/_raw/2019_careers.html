We're Just Getting Started
BRINGING THE WORLD CLOSER TOGETHER
So far, we've connected more than 2 billion people — which means we still have more than 5 billion to go. The scale and complexity of our work are unprecedented, and the work we do makes a real difference in the lives of people around the world. Over time, we’ve expanded the Facebook family to include Instagram, WhatsApp, Messenger, Augmented & Virtual Reality, and Facebook Connectivity — but when it comes to bringing the world closer together, we’re just getting started.

Connectivity

Through Facebook Connectivity, we’re working to bring access to the internet and all it has to offer to everyone on the planet—giving more people a voice, strengthening community bonds and creating economic opportunities.

Artificial Intelligence

We’re using AI to help people get the information that’s most relevant to them in the most natural way—for example, translating languages automatically and automatically captioning photos to help visually impaired people better picture the world around them.

Augmented & Virtual Reality

We're using AR and VR to build powerful new ways for people to experience the world, express themselves and connect with others. Our commitment to innovation ensures that users will continue to enjoy state of the art AR effects and VR experiences in immersive technologies moving forward.

How We Hire
At Facebook, we’re looking for talented people to do work that they love at all levels of the company. Our hiring process is straightforward.

Submit Resume

Apply online or send a resume to your recruiter. Note that the most successful candidates possess the required skills and experience for the role they’ve applied for.

Have a Conversation

If selected to advance in the process, you'll speak with someone on our recruiting team to learn more about the role you've applied for and what it's like to work at Facebook.

Interviews

Next comes a remote interview with a hiring manager or potential teammate. If selected to move forward, you’ll come on-site to interview with people you’ll be working with, after which a recruiter will contact you to discuss any next steps.
