How much of this language is gender-coded?

One square = one corpus sentence, scored on a gender-coding axis built from pure gender-term pairs (Kozlowski et al. 2019) and ordered top-to-bottom from most masculine-coded to most feminine-coded. Prototype on pilot data — instrument not yet judge-validated.
