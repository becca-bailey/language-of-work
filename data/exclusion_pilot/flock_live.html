Our Story
Our StoryExperience FlockEngineering & ProductOpen Roles

Turn Bold Ideas Into Thriving Communities

At Flock, building the impossible isn’t just something we say. It’s our daily commitment to creating safer, stronger communities. We bring together hardware, software and machine learning to help eliminate crime and empower those who protect and serve. Our work helps neighborhoods thrive, families feel secure and cities move forward with confidence.

Career Defining Moments Start Here

We’re a high-performance team fueled by optimism, urgency, and a shared belief that the hardest challenges are the ones worth taking on. That means big goals, bold thinking, and the freedom to go after problems in unconventional ways. We’ll be honest, working here is intense. You’ll be pushed to do the best work of your life, move fast, and consistently become 1% better every day. We don’t expect Flock to be the perfect place for everyone, but if you love hard work and having an impact, we could be a great fit.

Backed by Leading Investors

The People Behind the Mission

How We Operate

We believe in getting things done together. Our culture is low ego and high ownership: no task is beneath anyone and every role is critical to the whole. We move quickly, make clear decisions and hold ourselves accountable to what we commit to. When something needs doing, we roll up our sleeves and solve it as a team. Our work is interconnected, like an assembly system; each part relies on the others to function at its best. That means we prioritize clarity, trust and collaboration every step of the way.

The Values That Define Us

Optimism with a Plan

We set bold goals and pursue them with confidence, always guided by a clear and thoughtful plan to get there.

Hard Work Worth Doing

We give our all because the mission matters. This is meaningful work that makes communities safer and it’s worth every ounce of effort.

Act Like a First Responder

We act with urgency, professionalism and care. When something needs attention, we respond quickly and follow through with excellence.

Our story

Milestones That Matter

What started as a bold idea to make communities safer has grown into a movement. Along the way, we’ve built breakthrough technology, expanded nationwide and helped thousands of communities solve crime and thrive in entirely new ways.

2017
Flock, founded by Garrett Langley, Matt Feury & Paige Todd, joins YC S17, solves its first crime & launches to improve neighborhood safety.
2018
We hit 1M plates processed daily and debut branded boxes making us feel like a real, grown-up company.
2019
We grow our customer base, hit 10M images in a day, and launch our V2 LPR, delivering sharper performance to the communities we serve.
2020
We expand with law enforcement partners, help solve our first AMBER Alert, and grow the team to 150 employees across the country.
2021
We launch gunshot detection, achieve a $1B valuation, grow to 300+ employees, and expand into the private sector.
2022
We hit a $3.5B valuation, grow to 600+ employees and launch Flock OS, our integrated safety platform.
2023
Now valued at $4.8B, we help solve 7% of U.S. crime, and launch new products like video and a mobile app.
2024
Now solving 10% of U.S. crime - from reuniting 400+ missing people to launching tools like OS 911, Mobile Trailers, NightVision, and Solar Video—we’ve grown to 1,100+ employees nationwide.
2025
We’ve come a long way—but this is just the beginning. There’s so much more to build, and we’re just getting started.
