Founding Office & Workplace Operations — New York City · Ops · FullTime

A Foundational Company

Rilla is on a mission to index the offline world.

Today, we've built the leading sales coaching software for organizations doing sales offline. We have over 1000 customers, including The Home Depot, KKR, Neighborly, and PulteGroup, using our product to enable AI sales coaching across their organization.

We are backed by Google Ventures, Bessemer Ventures, Crew Capital, and Broom Ventures, along with others.

We're an in-office team in NYC with builders who operate like high-speed reinforcement learners. We obsess over our customers, move at a high velocity, are not afraid of failure, and look to maximize our real-world impact.

The Role

As Rilla scales, the office is the engine everything else runs on, and right now there's no one who owns it end to end. This is a founding role to build it.

You'll own the physical space, the daily experience of being in the office, and the systems that get new people productive from day one.

Today this work is scattered and manual. Your job is to turn office and workplace operations into a system that runs itself — so a team of builders working ~60 hours a week in person never has to think about whether the space, the food, the supplies, or the onboarding "just works."

It does, because you built it that way.

We're looking for someone who reaches for AI and automation before the manual grind, treats logistics as a system to engineer, and takes extreme ownership of a space and a team's daily experience.

You'll sit on our BizOps team and work across HR, finance, engineering, and leadership, the connective tissue that keeps everyone moving fast.

What You'll Do

  • Own day-to-day office operations end-to-end.

  • Manage vendor relationships — food & beverage, cleaning, facilities, building management and hold them accountable to timelines and quality

  • Keep the office stocked: order and restock supplies, snacks, drinks, and equipment before anyone notices they're low

  • Plan and run internal events, team offsites, celebrations, and the moments that build culture in a high-intensity in-office team

  • Own office-related budgets, expenses, and reconciliations, and find ways to spend smarter as we scale

  • Build the systems, checklists, and automations that make all of the above continuous instead of a scramble

  • Be the go-to person for anything space-, supply-, or experience-related, and the first line for keeping the office a place people want to be
