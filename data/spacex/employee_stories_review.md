In concert with the SpaceX leadership team, Adam works to secure financial support for SpaceX business activities and programs. He was most recently VP of Assembly and Physical Logistics responsible for all aspects of BMW 5 Series assembly and physical logistics at the company's plant in Shenyang, China. He has spent most of his 22-year automotive industry career in the United Kingdom, where he has served as assembly planning director, assembly production director, plant "Lean manufacturing" director, plant quality director and deputy VP for quality and engineering and assembly quality manager for BMW's MINI division.
- **Leadership** — As Vice President of Launch and Test, Mr. Buzza's responsibilities include launch and mission activities in Hawthorne, CA, integration and test work in McGregor, TX, and launch site operations at Cape Canaveral (FL) and Vandenberg (CA). Since joining SpaceX in 2002, Tim has led the development, testing and successful launch of the Falcon 1 launch vehicle, the Falcon 9 launch vehicle and the Dragon spacecraft. In addition, Tim led development of SpaceX launch sites at Cape Canaveral, Vandenberg, and the United States Army Kwajalein Atoll. Under Tim's direction, development of the Cape Canaveral launch site--from demolition to fully operational--was completed in less than 2 years. He also oversaw development of a state-of-the-art Mission Control Center at the company's Hawthorne headquarters to conduct on-orbit operations, as well as of a 700-acre Rocket Development and Test Facility in McGregor, TX, which features 11 different test stands used to certify vehicle components for flight. Prior to joining SpaceX, Tim was the test project manager for Boeing's Delta IV first stage. His experience includes structural testing of the liquid hydrogen and liquid oxygen tanks, engine section, interstage and nosecone, as well as analysis and verification of the booster and stage separation systems. Tim was responsible for schedule and budget performance on 23 major tests, with a total budget of $100 million. Tim has extensive test experience in the aerospace industry, ranging from aircraft systems testing (fuel, hydraulics and environmental) to stage development of a medium to heavy payload rocket. His career has taken him to major test sites in the United States and Europe. Tim has a Bachelor of Science and a Master of Science in mechanical engineering from the Pennsylvania State University. His work has been published by ASME, and he holds several US patents.

Gwynne was subsequently recruited to be Director of Microcosm’s Space Systems Division, where she served on the executive committee and directed corporate business development.

- **Leadership** — Mr. Mueller is one of the world's foremost rocket engine designers and is responsible for building and managing an elite propulsion development group at SpaceX. His department is responsible for all propulsion development at SpaceX, including propulsion systems and engines for the launch vehicles and Dragon spacecraft. Prior to joining SpaceX as a founding employee, Tom spent 15 years at TRW, where he ran the Propulsion and Combustion Products department and was responsible for liquid rocket engine development activities. During his career at TRW, he was the lead engineer for development of the TR-106 650,000-lbf thrust LOX/hydrogen engine. Tom has a broad range of rocket engine design, development and testing experience, including work with all common liquid propellants and many advanced propellants, providing thrust ranging from 5 lbf to 650,000 lbf. Tom has a Master of Science in mechanical engineering from Loyola Marymount University and a Bachelor of Science in mechanical engineering from the University of Idaho. He has received many awards, including the TRW Chairman's Award, the company's most prestigious award for technical achievement, and he holds several US patents in propulsion technology.

Mark holds a Bachelor of Arts in Economics and Master of Engineering in Systems Engineering, both from Cornell University where he was part of three winning Formula SAE teams.
