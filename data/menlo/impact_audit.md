# Menlo impact-claim denominator audit (Act 3 spine)

How Menlo quantifies its "impact," scanned across the 374-chunk firm corpus
(2026-06-24). The question H4 asks: is the denominator *story-consumption* (people who
came to look) or *practice-adoption* (workplaces that rebuilt on the model)?

| Denominator category | Hits | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| Books / speaking / teaching / workshops / keynotes | 111 | The megaphone — dominant self-description. |
| Visitors / tours / "came to visit" / guests | 56 | Paid curiosity; *Joy, Inc.* brags "2,197 visitors." |

## The named-adopter test (the linchpin)
Every match for adoption/replication language resolves to something *other* than an
organization rebuilding on the Menlo Way:
- *"software that is widely **adopted** and enjoyably used by their **end users**"*
  (2013, 2015, 2023, 2010×3) — their clients' **products** gaining user adoption; this
  is Menlo's value proposition, not cultural propagation.
- *"interns are **adopted** quickly and become treasured members of our team"* (2010).
- *"a major weakness… for Agile to be **adopted** by companies"* (2009 blog comment,
  about Agile generally — not Menlo).

**Finding:** in their own words, across 25 years, Menlo never names an organization that
rebuilt its workplace on the Menlo Way. Their notion of "impact" is *clients' product
adoption + tour visitors + book/keynote reach* — story-consumption, not practice-
adoption. The replication market does not visibly clear.

## Honesty guardrail
This measures the **absence of a documented/claimed adopter**, not proof of zero
influence (practices may have been carried home quietly). Claim stays narrow:
*the replication market did not visibly clear; Menlo is cited as an admired exception,
not a propagated template.* Calibrate against the Spotify squads/tribes foil (a model
that DID get named-adopted) to show this is a propagation measure, not a hypocrisy test.

## Cross-check: the outside view (worker register)
HN corpus (~40 items, 2009–2026): Menlo surfaces a few dozen times, framed as a rarity —
*"one of the few that get it," "companies that get it are hard to find — Pivotal and
Menlo," "people are paying to learn the Menlo Way."* Admired outlier / paid curiosity,
flat over time. Report as trajectory/qualitative (small N), never a statistic.
