Be Yourself, Every Day

Diversity, Inclusion, Empowerment

Millions of people from all walks of life use Snapchat every day to communicate with friends and family. It’s important to us to bring the same diversity of cultures, backgrounds, and perspectives together here at Snap Inc! We strive to cultivate a culture of inclusivity, and we’re investing in new ways to strengthen that culture every day — through employee resource groups, internal development programs, unconscious bias training, networking opportunities, recruiting initiatives, and more. We want to make sure that everyone comes to work knowing that they have a seat at the table and a voice that’s heard.

Employee Resource Groups

Our Employee Resource Groups are created and led by members of the Snap Inc. family. They empower us to come together to celebrate a common cause, raise awareness, encourage advocacy, and refine our approach to recruiting. Whether they’re throwing social events, hosting guest speakers, or spearheading new volunteer efforts, our Employee Resource Groups are always working to make a real difference — and real friends!

Lady Chillahs

Lady Chillahs supports, empowers, and advances women at Snap through workshops, outreach to women in need, and bringing the Snap family together to explore issues facing women today.

SnapNoir

​SnapNoir​ is a forum for fostering cultural understanding, diversity, and professional development for people of the African diaspora — both at Snap and in the community.

SnapPride

​SnapPride embraces and celebrates all genders, sexualities, and lifestyles! SnapPride focuses on empowering our local community, and increasing awareness and education surrounding LGBTQ+ issues.

¡Los​ Snaps!

​¡Los Snaps! empowers, uplifts, and celebrates the unique diversity that makes up the Latino community — while creating education and integration opportunities within the community here at Snap.

SnapVets

​SnapVets is a resource group for veterans, dependents, and those who support them. SnapVets empowers veteran professional development, community outreach, recruiting, and more!

SnapAsia

​SnapAsia is a community of people from Asian countries and the Pacific Islands, brought together to share our experiences, promote cultural understanding, and support one another through networking, professional development, and community outreach.

Veteran Fellowship Program

Our veterans offer unique skills, leadership, and perspectives that we value immensely here at Snap. However, the transition into a civilian workplace can sometimes be a challenge, so we’ve launched the Snap Inc. Veteran Fellowship program to help facilitate it! After the launch of the program, two-thirds of our Veteran Fellows received full-time offers — and all of them accepted. This program is just one part of a larger recruiting initiative to help cultivate stronger veteran representation here at Snap.
