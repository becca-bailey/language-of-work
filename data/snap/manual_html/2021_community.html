Giving Back

At Snap, there are tons of opportunities to make a lasting impact — both at work and in the community! Our giving programs focus around three key pillars: empowering youth, education, and the arts. We volunteer thousands of hours every year through Snap CAMP (Community, Arts, and Mentorship Projects). Whether we’re renovating houses for new mothers or offering free coding classes to women in need, we’re always finding new ways to give back. In addition to our sponsored volunteer projects, Snap team members can also take paid time each month to volunteer at a charity of their choice that aligns with our three giving pillars. We want to help you give back, your way!

Snap Council

Council is the practice of people coming together to share stories, listen deeply, and speak from the heart. Team members have the opportunity to speak without being interrupted, which ensures everyone has a chance to be heard. As stories are shared, others listen wholeheartedly. This fosters an inclusive work environment where people experience a sense of belonging. We are a global company that is solving complex problems — so it’s essential that we invite every voice into the conversation and deepen our ability to listen to one another.
