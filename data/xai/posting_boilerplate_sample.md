# Greenhouse posting boilerplate: deduped sample (xai)

Small era-spread sample of xAI job postings (Wayback captures of
boards.greenhouse.io/xai and job-boards.greenhouse.io/xai), fetched
2026-07-29 for the include/exclude decision flagged in
`discovery_report.md`. Raw HTML + full chunk texts: `posting_sample/`
(manifest.json). These are NOT in chunks/ or classifications.json —
repo precedent excludes job listings; Becca decides whether the shared
'About xAI' boilerplate below earns an exception.

Sample: 13 postings, 6 distinct boilerplate version(s).

## Version 1 (1 sightings, 20231105 – 20231105)

> xAI’s mission is to create AI systems that can accurately understand the universe and aid humanity in its pursuit of knowledge. Our team is small, highly motivated, and focused on engineering excellence. This is an organization for those who appreciate challenging themselves and who thrive on curiosity. Engineers are encouraged to work across multiple areas of the company, and as a result all engineers and researchers share the title “Member of Technical Staff”. We operate with a flat organizational structure. All employees are expected to be hands-on and to contribute directly to the company’s mission. Leadership is given to those who show initiative and consistently deliver excellence. Work ethic and strong prioritization skills are important. All engineers and researchers are expected to have strong communication skills. They should be able to concisely and accurately share knowledge with their teammates.
