# Anduril: the #DontWorkAtAnduril campaign (2024–2025)

Canon document for the exclusion story. Anduril's careers-page prose is
mission-forward, not overtly exclusionary — the exclusion register lives in
this out-of-home / social recruiting campaign (billboards showing
"Work at Anduril.com" with "Don't" spray-painted over it; launched early
2024, expanded 2025 to Boston/Atlanta/Seattle).

Only campaign copy verbatim-confirmed in press coverage is included. Sources:
Ad Age 2024 ("Anduril, the military contractor, has a message for potential
employees: Don't work here"), Entrepreneur 2025, PJX Media case study.
Register: employer-brand campaign (canon), not careers-page copy. The VP
Marketing gloss — "Anduril is not for everybody, that's the point" — is
interview speech, quoted in the memo but kept out of the copy block below.

---

Don't work at Anduril.

It's hard work, on hard problems, on hard mode. If that isn't for you, then
Anduril isn't the place for you.

We don't hire engineers. We recruit believers.
