# M4 read-through: mission_brand chunks by year

## 2010 (6 chunks)

- **Perspectives on Palantir** — Palantir places all the analytical power and technical prowess of the world’s best software engineers at your fingertips. Palantir shifts the paradigm away from both the etching technologies that merely let you map out what you already know and the quasi stand-alone field of artificial intelligence, that while useful for some types of analysis, has limits when it comes to making sense out of massive, complex, financial or intelligence information. We facilitate human-computer synthesis. You ask the questions and you visualize the data in ways that are useful to you and your mission. Through the user-friendly Palantir analytical platforms, the answers come forth just as if you had a team of top engineers working for hours to present you with the solutions to difficult problems. At present, the world of analysis is full of simple, linear tools that are built to go from A to B to C. Once built, these programs need massive infusions of cash and engineering to add any requirements changes. At Palantir, we’ve created a rich "conceptual environment," where each analyst can create his or her own visual intuitive space where they can absorb, understand, and interact with disparate information to come up with sophisticated findings. Much like a camera, the analyst can switch from a telephoto to a zoom lens to look at disparate bits of information, and divine their relationships. When necessary, analysts can leave their "space" and collaborate with other analysts, each sharing his or her interpretation of a data set while always maintaining the structural integrity of his or her own space. The intelligence or financial analyst can then make decisions based on the hard information that results from these real-time interactions with the data.
- **Perspectives on Palantir** — Over the last decade, Silicon Valley has transformed every individual with access to the Internet into a researcher. It used to be that you needed a Masters degree or a Ph.D. in a particular field, ties to highly specialized experts and tomes buried in dusty university libraries, to meet the myriad intellectual demands of work and life. Now — with a little help from Google and Wikipedia – anyone can sit down at a computer and tap into the vast knowledge base of experts who have spent weeks, years, even decades, researching a field. This transformation occurred so seamlessly that at first, we barely noticed its profound effects. However it can no longer be denied that in today’s work environment, being able to deepen our understanding of an issue has made us all the more valuable. Indeed, it is hard to imagine someone competing effectively without access to this ever-growing knowledge base. We at Palantir Technologies believe that the next seismic shift already has begun. We are being forced to answer questions reliably and expeditiously, armed not only with a researcher’s understanding of a large data set but also with an intelligence analyst’s sophisticated ability to find anomalous patterns upon which exceedingly important developments turn. However, those expectations can only be met with the right technology, technology that so far, has been lacking. At present, obtaining this level of reliable intelligence requires you to identify, recruit, and train highly qualified software engineers who are capable of writing the kind of algorithms needed to answer the complex questions you need asked and answered. Before Palantir, this was a daunting, expensive and time-consuming task.
