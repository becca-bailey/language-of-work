We're not for everyone, but we might be for you

At Engine, excellence is the standard. Our culture is not defined by any one person or team, but dictated by the collective force of the whole. The energy at Engine is electric.

Culture memo
Mission-first mindset
Operating principles

We give people high agency and real ownership.

The people who thrive here don't wait for permission. They move fast, hold themselves to a high bar, and make the work around them better.

This is not a place to coast. Complacency is our greatest threat, and we treat urgency as a default state. If that sounds like the environment you want, read on.

Our DNA connects us and propels us to achieve our goals

The future of Engine continues to get brighter and the opportunities are limitless. To navigate this future and truly seize the opportunities it presents, we lean on our DNA as the guiding principles that define who belongs here.

Customer Obsessed

Our success begins and ends with our customers. We deliver experiences that are effortless, reliable and delightful. Loyalty isn't locked in by contracts; it's earned through every interaction, every day. We win only when our customers win.

Drivers, not Passengers

At Engine, everyone takes the wheel. We act like owners, not bystanders, solving problems when we see them and carrying outcomes through to the finish. No task is beneath us, and no idea is too bold. If you're waiting for someone else, you're in the wrong seat.

Speed with Precision

The opportunity ahead of us is too big to slow down. We move fast without cutting corners. We have a bias to action, make quick decisions, and keep the bar high. Speed demands sharp judgment, decisiveness, and removing bureaucracy.

Insist on High Standards

We raise the bar in everything we do: our products, our work, and our customer interactions. We inspire and push each other to achieve more. Every seat must be earned, and underperformance is addressed quickly and respectfully.

Conviction with Rationale

We propose and challenge ideas with conviction, grounded in logic and communicated with clarity. We say what we mean and we say it clearly. We debate openly, but we stay curious, listen, adapt, and let the best ideas win. Progress requires alignment, not consensus.

Deliver Results

Execution is everything. We're always driving forward and never just spinning our wheels. We do this efficiently by cutting distractions, prioritising what moves the mission forward and eliminating what doesn't. We measure progress by impact, not effort.

Innovate & Elevate

Curiosity drives us to explore bold ideas and embrace the unknown. Innovation fuels our progress as a company, and personal growth drives our excellence as individuals. We question, experiment, learn fast, and continually master our craft to stay at the leading edge.
