# M4 read-through: mission_brand chunks by year

## 2014 (2 chunks)

## 2016 (13 chunks)

- **We're better together** — Our differences makes us stronger; inclusion makes them matter. That’s why building a strong culture is important to us — once you join the flock, we have many ways for you to get involved and bring your “whole self” to your role. Our employee groups foster supportive environments for employees of various backgrounds, and have also led the charge on incredible programming and initiatives, both internal and external. For example, @TwitterOpen helped us achieve an HRC Corporate Equality Index perfect score and our women’s groups developed our global 2016 #SheInspiresMe campaign around International Women’s Day . Through our #HeForShe team efforts, Twitter became the first corporate impact champion in tech for the UN’s global gender equality initiative. Thanks to the work of @Blackbirds , BuzzFeed named our custom fist Twitter emoji the best part of Black History Month, and our parents group helped expand our policy so all new parents can get up to 20 weeks of paid parental leave. Our technical women team up with programs like Girls Who Code to teach a new generation of curious and passionate thinkers. Learn more about our committment to inclusion and diversity.
- **Teaming up with our communities** — We work at Twitter because we're passionate about changing the world — and one of the ways we do this is by giving back to the communities where we live and work. Our Twitter for Good ( @twitterforgood ) programming includes training non-profits on how to harness the power of our platforms to get the word out about their work. Twice a year, we host a company-wide day of service called #FridayForGood where employees partner with community organizations to help our neighborhoods around the world. In addition to our local partnerships, we opened the Twitter @NeighborNest , our family-friendly learning center that cultivates technological literacy and a love for knowledge.
- **Where it all began** — Free speech. Creativity. Changing the world. These are ways to describe both Twitter and the history of San Francisco. Born on South Park Street in SoMa and now residing in the former SFMart building at 9th and Market, we have an incredible work space located in the heart of the City by the Bay. @TwitterSF is our global headquarters, so don't be surprised if you see celebrities, civil rights activists, musicians, politicians, and authors stopping by to talk about their work, share their Twitter stories, or Tweet a selfie.
- **Twitter is where inclusion lives** — People come to Twitter to freely express themselves, and we’ve seen this through individual Tweets and collective movements like #BlackLivesMatter , #ILookLikeAnEngineer , and #LoveWins . Just as inclusion lives on our platform, we’re striving to ensure Twitter is reflective of our users, and that our workplace and the decisions we make about it are equally inclusive. We want our different perspectives to flourish — they make us stronger and help make product decisions that will best serve our users around the world.
- **Our global offices** — We have over 35 offices around the world, and each one reflects the regional and cultural spirit of the cities they're in. Atlanta's conference rooms are named after southern hip-hop icons, local artists paint giant murals in our New York office, and Sydney has a panoramic view of the city and harbor from the 39th floor (make sure you check out the sunset ). Explore below to see all our offices and the job openings available.
- **Work on important problems.** — As a global real-time service, the Twitter platform requires a broad range of technical skills, from mobile and infrastructure to analytics and machine learning. Engineering here is challenging and fast paced.
- **TECHNOLOchicas** — @TwitterAlas empowers Latino employees to make an impact at Twitter and beyond, and the group recently teamed up with TECHNOLOchicas to focus on supporting a new generation of Latinas working in tech.
- **How we do our work** — We innovate, experiment, and move at a fast pace. We’re a learning organization with a growth mindset, and we’re always looking for ways to improve our product and ourselves.
- **How we collaborate** — Passion and personality matter. You’ll work with creative and curious people across the globe, and we want you to feel comfortable being yourself every day you're here.
- **Have the time of your life.** — People who work at Twitter say the biggest draw is their colleagues. You’ll find a generous, friendly team that is passionate about Twitter and the world at large.
- **How we approach the world** — We're building a platform where all voices can be heard, creating fun ways for people to express themselves, and growing our business in a way that makes us proud.
- **Lesbians Who Tech** — Our LGBTQ and women's groups help host the Lesbians Who Tech San Francisco Summit, including a keynote address, networking event, and career fair at Twitter HQ.
- **#SheInspiresMe** — Through this International Women's Day campaign, we honored the amazing women who’ve made a difference, both on and off Twitter, in ways big and small.
