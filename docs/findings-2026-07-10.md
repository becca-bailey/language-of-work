# Findings — 2026-07-10

Fresh analysis of today's data drops: AI mentions (16 companies), the craft-axis pilot
(Basecamp/Meta/Stripe), the AI-framing axis pilot (Google only), and the shape of the three
newly fetched corpora (Uber/Apple/Nvidia). Numbers computed directly from the data files
cited below; nothing here is from a prior write-up. Framing follows the counterforces
thesis (`docs/counterforces-thesis.md`); hedges are inline, not in a footnote.

## TLDR

- **The AI inflection is real but Salesforce-heavy.** Pooled prevalence: 1.6% (2023) → 3.4%
  (2024) → 12.5% (2025) → 13.2% (2026). Salesforce alone is 73 of 188 corpus AI chunks (39%);
  excluding it, 2024 stays flat at 1.8% and the jump lands in 2025 (8.0%) → 2026 (9.0%). The
  broad, multi-company inflection is a **2025** event, ~2.5 years after ChatGPT.
- **Hiring copy leads brand copy.** 2026 AI prevalence: job_listing 25%, mission_brand 11%,
  benefits_perks 0% in every year. Companies started *hiring for* AI harder than they
  *talk about* it — and nobody frames AI as a perk yet.
- **The zeros are as interesting as the spikes**: Basecamp 0/163 chunks 2024–26, Netflix 0,
  Amazon 0/21 in 2026, GitHub 0/28 in 2024–26 (Copilot's home!), Shopify 2/52 in 2026
  despite Tobi's famous AI-mandate memo. Careers pages lag or resist the internal reality.
- **Craft axis: Basecamp/Stripe genuinely craft-pole, but Meta's "drift toward craft" is
  probably a register artifact** — its post-2016 top-scoring chunks are inclusion/warmth
  language, not craftsmanship. Don't build the Meta motto-change story on this yet.
- **AI-framing axis (tool vs mandate) has no usable signal**: Google only, 44 chunks over
  21 years, most years n≤5. Salesforce is the obvious next company — it's where the
  "agentic coworkers" language actually lives.

## 1. AI mentions: who drives the inflection

Source: `data/<company>/ai_mentions.json` (16 companies; uber/apple/nvidia not yet
classified, excluded).

Pooled prevalence (AI chunks / total chunks): ≈0–2% every year 2005–2023, then
15/444 = **3.4% (2024)** → 67/537 = **12.5% (2025)** → 53/401 = **13.2% (2026)**.

**Salesforce concentration.** Salesforce has 73 corpus AI chunks; next are Google (45),
Meta (22), Stripe (15). Salesforce's own arc: 1 mention (2017), 1 (2023), then 8/53 = 15%
(2024), 36/149 = 24% (2025), 27/112 = 24% (2026). Recompute the pooled series without
Salesforce and 2024's "inflection" vanishes (1.8%, same as 2022–23); the rise becomes
2025: 8.0%, 2026: 9.0%. So: **2024 = Salesforce pivoting its careers site to "the #1 AI
CRM"; 2025 = the industry following.** What's behind Salesforce's 73: it's product
rebranding flowing into recruiting copy — "Agentforce" job families, "AI-first" as an
employer-brand adjective — not a new employee value proposition.

**Registers (talk vs hire).** Pooled prevalence by content label:

| label | pre-2024 | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| job_listing ("hire") | 2.4% | 8.2% | 17.0% | **25.0%** |
| mission_brand ("talk") | 1.0% | 1.8% | 10.3% | 11.3% |
| employee_story | 1.2% | 8.3% | 29.6% | 30.8% |
| benefits_perks | 0% | 0% | 0% | 0% |

Hire leads talk roughly 2:1. employee_story is the highest rate but tiny n (13–48
chunks/yr) — one Salesforce "your agentic coworkers" testimonial moves it a lot.

**Terms.** Overwhelmingly the bare word "AI" (275 of ~330 term hits 2024+); "machine
learning" collapsed from the dominant pre-2024 term (52 total) to 21. "Agentforce"/
"agentic" language is visible in Salesforce examples but isn't in the term lexicon —
worth adding before the next run. *(Done same day: "agentic" + "agentforce" added to
`src/lowork/ai_net.py` and the tracker re-run — corpus AI chunks 188 → 197. Numbers
elsewhere in this report were computed pre-change and may be off by a few chunks in
2024–26; the shape of every claim is unaffected.)*

**First-mention years** (unreliable until SPA-era gaps are filled — a company's "first
mention" is often just its first *captured* year with a job listing): Google 2005
("machine learning" in an internship listing — ML-as-jargon long predates the boom),
Palantir 2010, Salesforce/Stripe 2017, Meta 2019, GitLab/HubSpot 2025, Snap 2026.

**Evidence** (all from `ai_mentions.json` `examples`):
> "Here at Salesforce, we're innovators and lifelong learners. Together, we chart new
> paths, drive customer success, develop AI-first technology, and build a better world."
> — salesforce 2024, mission_brand
>
> "…when you rejoin you'll have Agentforce at your fingertips. Your agentic coworkers will
> surface smarter insights, streamline your day-to-d[ay]…" — salesforce 2025, employee_story
>
> "You'll lead design for Agentic Commerce… reimagine the consumer checkout experience with
> agentic commerce capabi[lities]" — stripe 2026, job_listing

**The zeros.** Basecamp 0 AI chunks ever, on a real n (163 chunks 2024–26) — a genuine
stance, consistent with the founders' public skepticism. Netflix 0 (31 chunks 2024–26).
Amazon 0/36 2024–26 — surprising; may be a corpus-slice issue (which Amazon pages are
captured?) rather than reality. GitHub 0/28 2024–26 despite Copilot — n is modest and
GitHub's 2025–26 capture is thin (7 and 5 chunks), so treat as a coverage question first,
finding second. Meta 2026 is 11/11 = 100%, but that is 11 chunks — say "all captured 2026
Meta chunks mention AI," not "100% prevalence."

## 2. Craft axis pilot (Basecamp, Meta, Stripe)

Source: `data/{basecamp,meta,stripe}/axis_scores.parquet` (axis=craft, chunk level;
positive = craft pole, negative = iteration/move-fast pole), `evidence_quotes.json`,
`validation.json`.

- **Basecamp**: craft-positive nearly every year (mean raw_topk_mean **+0.038** across
  2009–2026). Single negative year: 2024 (−0.012, n=17) — one year, don't interpret.
- **Stripe**: craft-positive (mean **+0.041**), strongest early (2012: +0.079, 2013: +0.145
  — but n=2 and n=1) and solid in well-covered years (2019: +0.094, n=30; 2022: +0.063, n=17).
- **Meta**: mean ≈ **0.000**. Iteration-pole early (2009: −0.059, 2010: −0.036; 2009–13
  mean −0.019), mildly positive after 2016 (2016–26 mean +0.010). Direction matches the
  2014 "move fast with stable infrastructure" story, **but** the top craft-scoring Meta
  chunks post-2016 are inclusion/warmth language ("People are at the heart of every
  connection we build", "Be your authentic self… a workplace where everyone belongs"), not
  craftsmanship. The drift is plausibly the *register mix* changing (more warm
  mission_brand copy), not craft language appearing. Yearly n is also tiny (1–16 chunks).

**Evidence** (`evidence_quotes.json`, key `craft`, chunk level):
> "Quality, clarity, attention to detail, and usefulness are at the core of everything we
> do." — basecamp 2009 (+0.14)
>
> "We're looking for engineers and designers who care deeply about quality of execution…"
> — stripe 2012 (+0.15)
>
> "We need help from people who like to move fast, tackle the big stuff, and have an
> immediate impact" — meta 2009 (−0.05, iteration pole)

Sentence-level top quotes include context-free fragments ("Working at Facebook means doing
what you love.", "For contractor opportunities at Facebook, click here.") — use chunk-level
quotes for anything published.

**Axis separation** (`validation.json` → `axis_separation`, craft vs performance): the two
axis *vectors* are nearly orthogonal (cosine 0.093), so the concepts are distinct by
construction. But Basecamp's chunk-level scores correlate at **r=0.657** (n=374). Honest
read: that's topical, not conceptual — Basecamp's corpus alternates "how we build
software" manifesto text with logistics/benefits text, and any work-practice chunk scores
above baseline on *both* axes; the correlation reflects what a chunk is about, not axis
redundancy. Supporting evidence: the same axis pair on Meta gives r=−0.04 (separated) and
Stripe r=0.15. Report the cosine as the separation claim; disclose the Basecamp r.

## 3. AI-framing axis (tool vs mandate) — no signal yet

Source: `data/google/ai_language_scores.parquet`, `data/google/ai_evidence.json`.
Google only; 44 scored chunks across 21 years; 13 of 21 year-rows flagged `thin`
(n≤3); best years are n=15 (2025), n=7 (2024). **This is not enough to say anything.**

For what it's worth (very little): 2024 mean −0.032 (mandate-lean), 2025 +0.016
(tool-lean), and Google's chunks are almost all "talk" (mission_brand) — AI-for-good
copy ("AI can help doctors and patients detect diseases earlier…", +0.057), not workplace
AI expectations. The construct this axis wants — "AI is a tool you may use" vs "AI use is
expected of you" — barely appears in Google's captured pages. Salesforce's Agentforce
copy and Shopify (post-memo) are where a mandate pole would actually show up.

## 4. New corpora: what stories they can(not) support

Fetched today, unclassified: `data/{uber,apple,nvidia}/chunks/*.jsonl`.

- **Uber, 158 chunks, 2011–2025.** 2020–2023 are 1 chunk each and 2025 is 1 chunk (SPA
  holes). But the 2017 values-rewrite window is bracketed: 2014–16 = 31 chunks before,
  2018–19 = 78 after (2017 itself only 4). **Supports**: a before/after comparison of the
  Kalanick-era values ("always be hustlin'") vs the 2017 Khosrowshahi rewrite — a clean
  scandal-driven (not labor-market-driven) canon change, a useful contrast case for the
  thesis. **Cannot support**: anything about 2020–23 (COVID, driver-classification fights)
  or the AI era until the SPA years are manually captured.
- **Apple, 971 chunks, continuous 2005–2026.** The best-shaped corpus in the project —
  every year covered, 100+ chunks/yr 2021–23. **Supports**: full axis trajectories, and a
  strong AI-mentions null: crude keyword pass finds AI terms in only ~4/98 2025 chunks —
  if classification confirms, Apple is a big-cap counterexample to the 2025 surge
  (consistent with its secrecy norm). Run the standard classify→AI-mentions pass first.
- **Nvidia, 220 chunks.** Hole exactly where it hurts: 2019 and 2021–23 have **zero**
  chunks (2020: 13) — the deep-learning gold-rush years. Heavy 2025–26 coverage (108
  chunks). **Supports**: early-era (2006–18) vs AI-era (2024–26) contrast. **Cannot
  support**: "when did Nvidia's language turn" timing claims — the turn is inside the hole.
  Manual Wayback capture for 2019–23 is the unlock, same playbook as Meta/Snap.

## 5. Context: where this sits against the established arcs

From `astro/src/data/stories/{dei,performance,wellbeing,power}.json`: DEI adopted then
retracted post-2023 (counter-registers overtake by 2025); performance language flat ~0.9
throughout with hardening stance; wellbeing spiked with the Great Resignation then
deflated. The AI-mentions surge is the first *new* language wave the corpus has caught
arriving in the low-leverage era — and per the register split it arrives as employer
*demand* (job listings) rather than worker *benefit* (0% in benefits_perks), which is
what the counterforces thesis predicts for language adopted when workers lack leverage.
That's a co-occurrence observation, not a causal claim.
