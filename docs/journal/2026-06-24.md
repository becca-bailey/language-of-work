# June 24, 2026

I think I arrived at a good thesis. Throughout the 2010s, we focused on culture when we should have been focusing on power.

We focused on DEI committees, not labor protection. We focused on collaboration, surveys, and culture rather than collective action. It looked effective while workers had some bargaining power brought on by plentiful jobs and talent scarcity, but quickly went away after those factors were gone.

I'm curious about what other archaeology we can do to track changes in worker power.

I'm curious what we can learn about hiring processes and how they have changed as worker power has declined.
