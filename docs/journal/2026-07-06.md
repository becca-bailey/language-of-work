Data analysis involves a lot of judgement calls. For example, is PTO a benefit that falls on an organization or an individual to protect? In my experience, most of the time individuals have to defend it, make up for it, leave detailed instructions, and explain to their managers why work didn't get done in their absence. So cynically, I'm calling it individual, but it's a distinction that's hard to see from the outside.
