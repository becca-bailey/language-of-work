## How to read these charts

- **What counts as a match:** each Netflix concept and its paraphrases are
  embedded; a company sentence that lands above the validated similarity
  threshold and survives an LLM-judge check against the concept counts.
- **Lifts vs. echoes:** a lift is above-threshold borrowing; an echo is
  adjacent language below the threshold that the judge still accepts as the
  same idea.
- **What's excluded:** "dream team" (it caught interchangeable
  "amazing team" boilerplate), "raise the bar" / "only the best" (industry
  convergence, Amazon's register), and anything a company said before 2009.
  Basecamp preached simplicity before the deck existed and sits in the
  cohort as the control case.
