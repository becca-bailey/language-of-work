Scores are z-scored within each company, so the question is when a company's own copy got
more or less idealistic — not whether Google sounds loftier than Amazon. Compare shapes and
timing, not absolute levels.

Sentence-level scoring surfaces the most idealistic lines per year ("Can one conversation
change the world?") rather than diluting them with navigation chrome. When idealism drops
alongside the control axis, the signal may be page composition — not rhetoric.
