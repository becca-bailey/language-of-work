## How to read these charts

- **The AI tracker** is the share of careers-page text mentioning AI at
  all: under 2% for two decades, then 12–13% industry-wide in 2025–26.
- **Careers pages are not product strategy.** Salesforce ran both
  vocabularies at once (craft +0.05 → +0.08 while AI went ~1% → 25%);
  GitHub ships Copilot yet mentions AI in zero captured careers chunks
  from 2024–26; Basecamp drifted off the craft pole while mentioning AI
  zero times in 163 chunks. The register has its own inertia.
- **What would settle the question:** two or three more years of pages,
  the Nvidia and Uber archive holes filled, and the tool-versus-mandate
  framing axis — which today finds even Salesforce's dense AI copy
  balanced between "AI as something we give you" and "AI as something we
  expect of you."
