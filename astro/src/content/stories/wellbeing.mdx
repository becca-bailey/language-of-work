## What this can't say

- The aggregate individualization index this study was designed around is
  too sparse to test: only 8 of 15 companies have enough enumerated
  benefits on both sides of 2022, at 2–5 items each.
- Benefits data throughout is self-presentation, not provision.
- The strong claim is about language only: a worker-concession bundle
  (care + DEI talk) that inflated with the labor market and deflated as
  leverage returned, while performance language never moved. The benefit
  shapes are texture — vivid where clean, silent where the counts run out.
